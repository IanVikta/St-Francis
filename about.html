Our Story

Educating Minds, Inspiring Hearts Since 1989

St. Francis Day and Boarding Primary School – Ntinda is a vibrant, inclusive primary school founded in 1989 along Kiwatule Road, dedicated to providing holistic, high-quality Nursery and Primary education to children across Kampala and beyond.

1989

St. Francis Day and Boarding Primary School was founded in Ntinda with a vision to provide accessible, high-standard foundational education to the community.

1998

Expanded campus facilities to introduce full boarding dormitories for boys and girls alongside a growing Upper Primary candidate section.

2010

Commissioned our modern classroom complex, school library, and ICT learning resource center to equip learners with 21st-century literacy skills.

2025

Celebrating over 36 years of academic excellence, strong UNEB PLE Division One performance, and thousands of proud alumni building the nation.

Our Mission

To provide learners with knowledge and skills for self-reliance thus enabling them face challenges of a modern society.

Our Vision

To produce an all-round learner.

Core Values

Academic Excellence

We believe that every student has the potential to achieve great things, and that we should strive to provide a high-quality education that prepares them for success in all areas of life.

Community Partnership

We foster close collaboration between parents, teachers, and the wider Ntinda community to nurture responsible, empathetic citizens.

Creativity & Talent

Learning is engaging, interactive, and joyful—encouraging pupils to explore sports, music, arts, and practical innovation.

Discipline & Integrity

We instill strong moral values, God-fearing character, self-reliance, and respect for self and others in every learner.
