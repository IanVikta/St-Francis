Discover Our Learning Sections

At St. Francis Day and Boarding Primary School – Ntinda, we follow the National Curriculum Development Centre (NCDC) framework enhanced with learner-centered instruction, regular continuous assessments, and individualized remedial support from Nursery through Primary Seven.

Strong phonics, reading fluency, and numeracy foundation in Early Years & Lower Primary
Experienced, dedicated teachers for English, Mathematics, Integrated Science, and Social Studies
Practical computer literacy, library reading hours, and co-curricular talent development
