Privacy & Data Protection Policy

St. Francis Day and Boarding Primary School – Ntinda is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our pupils, parents, guardians, staff, and website visitors in accordance with the Data Protection and Privacy Act of Uganda.

1. Introduction

When you submit an admission application, book a campus visit, or contact St. Francis Day and Boarding Primary School – Ntinda through our website, you entrust us with personal family and pupil records. We treat this information with strict confidentiality.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how parents or guardians can review or update their records with our School Administration Office.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to process admissions, deliver educational services, and communicate with parents and guardians:

2.1 Information Provided by Parents & Guardians

When completing online forms (Admissions Application, Tour Booking, Contact Inquiry, or Newsletter), you may provide:

  • Parent/guardian names, phone numbers, residential addresses, and email addresses
  • Pupil full name, date of birth, gender, previous school records, and birth certificate copies
  • Emergency contact details and relevant medical/immunization information
  • Passport photographs and supporting admission documents

2.2 Technical Website Information

Our website automatically logs standard technical metadata to maintain site security and reliability:

  • Browser type and device screen size
  • Date and time of form submissions
  • Basic anonymous page visit analytics

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted to St. Francis Day and Boarding Primary School – Ntinda is used exclusively for legitimate school operations:

  • Evaluating and processing pupil admission applications
  • Communicating interview dates, admission decisions, and termly school circulars
  • Safeguarding pupil health and welfare in our day and boarding sections
  • Complying with Ministry of Education and Sports (MoES) and UNEB registration requirements

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We never sell, rent, or trade pupil or parent personal data to third parties. Information is shared only in the following circumstances:

4.1 Educational & Regulatory Authorities

Where required for official pupil registration and national examinations, necessary pupil details are submitted to the Ministry of Education and Sports (EMIS) and the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB).

4.2 Medical Emergencies & Legal Obligations

Relevant health records may be shared with qualified medical personnel in the event of a medical emergency involving a pupil, or where required by Ugandan law.

5. Data Security

We implement administrative and technical safeguards to protect uploaded admission documents and inquiry records:

  • Uploaded admission documents and records are restricted from public web browsing
  • Access to pupil and parent records is restricted to authorized admissions and administrative staff
  • Physical files at our Ntinda campus are stored securely in the Headteacher's and Registrar's offices

6. Parent & Guardian Rights

Parents and legal guardians have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information held about their child
  • Update or correct contact numbers, emergency contacts, or medical records
  • Unsubscribe from optional school newsletter emails at any time
  • Request deletion of incomplete or withdrawn online admission inquiries

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect school policy improvements or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
