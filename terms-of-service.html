Website & Admissions Portal Terms

Please read these terms carefully before submitting online applications, tour bookings, or using resources on the St. Francis Day and Boarding Primary School – Ntinda website.

Authentic Records
Child Safeguarding
School Code of Conduct

Online Admissions

Submitting an online application form initiates the admission review process and does not constitute automatic placement until verified by the Admissions Office.

  • Accurate pupil & guardian details
  • Authentic birth & academic records
  • Placement assessment attendance
  • Official admission letter issuance

Parent Responsibilities

Parents and guardians using our portal agree to provide truthful information and adhere to school policies upon enrollment.

  • Timely disclosure of medical needs
  • Adherence to visiting day schedules
  • Respectful community communication
  • Compliance with school fee policies

Prohibited Use

No submission of falsified academic or birth documents
No unauthorized reproduction of pupil photographs
No misuse of the Staff & Admin login portal
No abusive or inappropriate comments in the school gallery

Key Policies to Note

Term Dates & Schedules

School term dates and event schedules published on this website are subject to Ministry of Education and Sports (MoES) national calendar adjustments.

Child Protection & Media

Photographs featured on our website and gallery celebrate school life. Parents may notify the Headteacher's office regarding media consent preferences.

Intellectual Property

The St. Francis Day and Boarding Primary School – Ntinda badge, motto, and website content are the property of the school.

Official Fee Payments

All school fees and admission payments should only be made through designated St. Francis Primary School bank accounts or official bursar payment channels.

Verification of Documents

Original birth certificates, immunization cards, and previous term report cards must be presented during the physical admission interview.

Policy Updates

The School Management Committee reserves the right to update these website terms and school regulations as needed.

Need Clarification on Admissions or School Rules?

Our Admissions Registrar and Headteacher's Office at Ntinda, Kiwatule Road are happy to assist you.
