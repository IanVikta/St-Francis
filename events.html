Annual Practical Science & Innovation Fair

09:00 AM - 04:00 PM

Main School Hall & Science Courtyard

Primary Four to Primary Seven pupils present working models in renewable energy, water purification, simple machines, and agricultural science. Parents and guest judges are warmly invited.

22 JUN

Parents Visitation & Academic Consultation Day

09:00 AM - 05:00 PM

Main Campus Classrooms & Grounds

An opportunity for parents and guardians to review Mid-Term assessments with class and subject teachers, inspect pupils' exercise books, and spend quality family time with boarding pupils.

Learn More
30 JUN

Inter-House Athletics & Sports Gala Finals

08:30 AM - 04:30 PM

St. Francis Main Sports Field

Cheer on St. Francis, St. Clare, St. Anthony, and St. Theresa Houses as pupils compete in sprint relays, sack races, football, netball, tug-of-war, and marching displays.

Learn More
05 JUL

Music, Dance & Drama Inter-House Festival

10:00 AM - 04:00 PM

Main School Amphitheater

A colourful celebration of Ugandan traditional folk dances, choral verse, western set pieces, original skits, and instrumental ensembles performed by our Nursery and Primary pupils.
