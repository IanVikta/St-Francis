St. Francis Old Students

Once a Franciscan, Always Part of the Family

Since 1989, thousands of boys and girls have begun their academic journey at St. Francis Day and Boarding Primary School – Ntinda. Our Old Students Association brings together former pupils to mentor current P.7 candidates, reconnect with classmates, and support school community initiatives.
