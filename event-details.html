Annual Practical Science & Innovation Fair

Join us at St. Francis Day and Boarding Primary School – Ntinda for our flagship Annual Practical Science & Innovation Fair. Designed to bring the National Curriculum Development Centre (NCDC) Integrated Science syllabus to life, this exhibition showcases creative, pupil-built projects from Lower and Upper Primary classes.

Parents, guardians, and prospective families are invited to tour the exhibition stalls, interact with young scientists as they explain their experiments, and attend the award ceremony recognizing the most innovative class projects.

Exhibition Highlights

  • Renewable Energy & Simple Machines (P.6 & P.7): Working solar water heaters, wind turbine models, pulleys, and electric circuit boards.
  • Water Filtration & Sanitation (P.4 & P.5): Domestic sand-and-charcoal water filters, oral rehydration preparation, and handwashing innovations.
  • Urban School Gardening & Nutrition (P.1–P.3): Sack gardening, seed germination stages, and balanced diet food displays.
  • ICT & Scratch Coding Corner: Interactive educational animations and quizzes created in our computer laboratory.
