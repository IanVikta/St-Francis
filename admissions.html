Begin Your Child's Academic Journey at St. Francis

Discover a nurturing, disciplined, and high-achieving educational environment at St. Francis Day and Boarding Primary School – Ntinda. Whether your child is starting in Daycare & Kindergarten or joining our Lower and Upper Primary classes (P.1–P.7), our admissions team is ready to guide your family every step of the way.

How to Apply

1

Submit Application Form

Complete our online admission form or pick up a physical form from the Admissions Office at our Ntinda, Kiwatule Road campus.

2

Provide Required Documents

Attach copies of the child's birth certificate, immunization card, recent passport photos, and previous school report cards (for P.1–P.7 applicants).

3

Placement Assessment & Interview

Prospective pupils attend a friendly age-appropriate readiness check or written placement interview in Mathematics, English, Science, and Social Studies.

4

Admission Letter & Enrollment

Successful applicants receive an official Admission Letter, circular of term requirements, uniform checklist, and bank payment slips to confirm their place.

Admission Requirements

Previous Academic Report Cards

Applicants joining Primary 1 to Primary 7 must present their most recent end-of-term report card from their previous school.

Birth Certificate & Passport Photos

A copy of the child's birth certificate (or immunization card) and two recent passport-size photographs of the pupil and parent/guardian ID.

Medical & Immunization Record

Updated immunization record and disclosure of any allergies or special medical needs so our school nurse and matrons can care for your child.

Placement Assessment

Satisfactory performance in our oral readiness interaction (Nursery) or written placement test (Lower and Upper Primary).
