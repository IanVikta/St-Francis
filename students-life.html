A Safe & Caring Home Away From Home

Our boarding section for Primary Three to Primary Seven pupils provides a structured, family-like environment supervised by experienced full-time matrons, wardens, and resident teachers.

Separate Boys & Girls Dormitories

Spacious, well-ventilated dormitories with treated mosquito nets, clean showers, and 24/7 matron care

Nutritious Balanced Meals

Freshly prepared breakfast, break-time porridge, lunch, evening tea, and supper including fresh fruits

Supervised Evening Prep

Guided homework and revision sessions every evening with subject teachers on duty

Weekend Recreation & Worship

Saturday laundry inspection, games, talent shows, and Sunday chapel/faith services
